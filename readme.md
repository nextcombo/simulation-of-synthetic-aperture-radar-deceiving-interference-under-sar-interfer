# Simulation of Synthetic Aperture Radar Deceiving Interference under SAR Interference with Doppler Algorithm [Attached MATLAB Code]

The direct-path deception interference in enemy radar detection processes can achieve the goal of misleading enemy decision-making and protecting our target by creating specific false scenes. The deception interference signal can be considered as the delayed transmission of radar signals, exhibiting a strong phase correlation with the true target signal. Therefore, it is difficult to directly distinguish and filter out during the SAR imaging process, resulting in the real echo signal and deception interference signal obtaining similar signal processing gains during processing. This leads to the mixing of the real target and the interference scene in the imaging results, directly affecting the perception performance of the radar monitoring equipment, making it impossible to accurately read the information of the real scene.
The direct-path fake target deception interference signal utilizes the fake target interference signal generated by DRFM interference devices [46]. Its generation process is summarized as follows: Firstly, the interference device intercepts the enemy's SAR signal and conducts reconnaissance on the signal parameters and carrier aircraft movement parameters; then, based on the preset interference position, the interference device system sets its response function, modulates the intercepted SAR signal through DRFM; finally, the deception interference signal is forwarded to the radar receiver, allowing for the creation of a false point target. To create a false scene deception interference, one could consider the entire scene as a combination of multiple scattering points, using DRFM interference devices to modulate different response functions on the intercepted radar signal and to combine and forward the generated interference signals.
